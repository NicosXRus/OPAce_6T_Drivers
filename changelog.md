## New version - 1.6.3
- Sources Drivers updated from ColorOS 16.0.10.500 (compatible with 16.0.10.600)
- New WebUI!
- New mounting mode - Metamodule (auto)
- Updated OverlayIMG and OverlayData. They now use xattr flags for directories, and the overlay now covers the entire ODM.
- Fix bugs

We are preparing for the launch of ColorOS 17 and module version 3.0🎉
